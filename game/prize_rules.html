Weekly Prize Lap: Official Rules

No purchase necessary. Buying anything does not help you win. This is a contest of skill. Void where prohibited.

1. Who runs it

Internet Motor Speedway (@internetmotorspeedway on Instagram), Los Angeles, California. Instagram does not sponsor, run or endorse this contest.

2. Who can enter

You must be 18 or older, live in the United States, and follow @internetmotorspeedway on Instagram (following is free). You confirm your age in the game before you drive. The people who run the Speedway and their households cannot win.

3. How to enter

Open the game, tap Race now, then Weekly Prize Lap, and drive. It is free. There is no entry fee and nothing to buy. Pit Crew membership, upgrades and custom cars make no difference: everyone drives the same stock car on the Speedway Oval. You can drive as many laps as you like. One Instagram account per person.

4. When

Each week starts Monday at 12:00 am and ends Sunday at 11:59 pm, Pacific time. Only laps that reach our server inside that week count for that week.

5. How the winner is picked

The driver with the single fastest lap of the week wins. Every lap is replayed and checked by our server before it counts. If two drivers set the same time, the lap that was set first wins. No random draw is involved.

6. The prize

One prize a week: $25 (US dollars), paid by the Speedway by PayPal, Venmo or Cash App. No substitutes. The winner is responsible for any taxes.

7. How we contact the winner

We send the winner a direct message from @internetmotorspeedway within 3 days after the week ends. We will never ask for a password or for money. The winner has 7 days to answer and tell us where to send the prize. If we get no answer, or the winner cannot accept it, the prize goes to the next fastest eligible driver.

8. Fair play

We can disqualify any lap or driver, at our sole judgment, for cheating, changing the game, using bots or scripts, using more than one account, or anything that gives an unfair edge. A lap our server cannot confirm does not count. Our decisions are final.

9. Your name

By entering, you agree that we can show your Instagram username and lap time on the leaderboard and in our posts about the contest.

10. The rest

We can pause, change or end the contest at any time, for example if the game or server has a problem. If a week is ended early, the prize goes to the fastest checked lap up to that point. We are not responsible for lost signal, crashes or laps that do not reach the server. California law applies.
